# Capacity-feedback comparison: original v8 versus v8.1

This is an offline diagnostic comparison of four models on matched seeds 11/41,
not a change to historical leaderboard scores. All 16 worlds completed 60 ticks
with clean integrity, full usage coverage and ready study manifests. The
dashboard name v8.1 is the display alias for participant-v8-revised.

The current dashboard branch contains an older static catalog/database (v6)
and discovers these newer jobs directly. That database was queried and verified;
this analysis therefore uses the ready job manifests and hashed artifacts,
not the stale static leaderboard. No simulations, model calls or evidence edits
were made.

## Comparable scoring

- Capability: original-population mean health over all 60 completed ticks,
  with deaths contributing zero and starting tick zero excluded.
- Execution: successful actions/messages divided by submitted non-contention
  actions/messages; skipped proposals and malformed model outputs earn no credit.
- Production: the unchanged productive-value-added formula per 100 original
  population agent-ticks. Pool raw counts across seeds before scoring.

Original v8 used final-season-weighted Capability and whole-decision Execution.
Comparing the displayed historical scores directly to v8.1 would conflate
scoring with changed behavior. We reconstruct old per-action outcomes by replaying
the fixed recorded decisions through the pinned 783341a engine. Every replay
matches all terminal snapshot fields generated by the engine, every living-agent
health observation, and the entire health trajectory. Post-fix replays also
match the recorded per-action telemetry exactly. Original ledgers remain untouched.
This extends the earlier audit: per-action scores cannot be recovered from
aggregate old error counts alone, but deterministic replay recovers them here.

## Interpretation

Capability and Production improve in all eight matched seed pairs; Execution
improves in seven. GPT-5.5 seed 41 is the exception: execution falls from 83.88
to 81.87, and capacity failures increase from 49 to 61. Thus the fix does not
eliminate inventory mistakes or guarantee a gain in every run.

Mini has the largest pooled execution improvement (+11.97 points). Terra and
Mini gain about 47% and 50% Production, respectively; Luna gains about 37%,
5.5 about 17%. Luna gains the most Capability (+7.24 points), with final
survivors rising from 1/20 to 8/20. Across these four models, capacity failures
fall from 778 to 367 (about 53%), despite more total resolved decisions.

Terra’s gain mainly reflects extraction: gathering accounting output rises
from 946 to 1419 and chopping from 132 to 309. Its farm harvest output falls
from 150 to zero, and completed farms fall from five to two (the old cohort
also built one shelter). Mini builds no structures in either condition.
Luna completes one versus two farms, but harvest output falls from 10 to 6.
5.5 completes four versus six farms plus one new storage, while farm harvest
output is nearly flat at 236 versus 232. Production rewards productive output
from gathering as well as infrastructure; these gains do not establish more
complex industry or greater commercial sophistication.

## Attribution and limitations

Within every matched pair, model, provider, medium effort, full world config
and static prompt hashes match. Original source is 75bd330; patched sources
are 783341a and 39232e4. The simulation changes are capacity/zero-quantity
feedback and private per-action telemetry; source also includes scoring and
operational/finalization changes. Exact replay supports unchanged physical
resolution for the observed actions. Feedback necessarily changes later
observations, which is the intended treatment.

The pattern is consistent with corrected feedback improving behavior and is
not merely a score-formula artifact. It is still a historical before/after
comparison: model responses are stochastic, runs occurred at different times,
and matched world seeds do not fix provider randomness. Two worlds per model
do not establish precise causal effects, and the eight pairs are not eight
independent model families. No p-value or tight confidence interval is claimed.
The restored broad Capability ordering does not by itself validate health as
a comprehensive capability index; 5.5 and Mini are separated by only 0.53
pooled health points, with the ordering reversing on seed 41.

## Coverage and reproducibility

- GPT-5.6 Sol: excluded. No original-v8 pre-fix study; v6 differs in horizon and prompts.
- Muse Spark 1.2: excluded. Original-v8 readiness diagnostic_only; revised study lacks ready status.
- Gemini 3.6/3.7: excluded. No matched pre-fix v8 studies; revised jobs flagged needs_provenance_review.

Additional post-fix models cannot answer a pre/post question without an
equivalent pre-fix baseline. V6 is contextual evidence, not a substitute.

SHA-256 hashes of each report, event ledger, usage ledger, snapshot and manifest.
